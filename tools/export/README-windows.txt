World (Windows, 64-bit)

Run:   World.exe
       Windows may warn that it doesn't recognise the app (it isn't signed):
       choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

Needs: Windows 10 or 11 with a DirectX 12 or Vulkan graphics driver.

Keep world_kernels.dll next to World.exe (it runs the simulation's
heavy passes; without it the game falls back to slower built-in ones).

Controls are listed along the bottom of the screen. T switches the look.
