World (Linux, x86_64)

Run:   ./World.x86_64
       (or double-click it; if your file manager asks, choose "Run")

Needs: a graphics driver with Vulkan (most Linux desktops from 2019 on;
       Mesa for Intel/AMD, the proprietary driver for NVIDIA), and
       glibc 2.34 or newer for the fast native simulation passes
       (Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Fedora 35+). On older systems the
       game still runs, with those passes in its slower built-in form.

Keep libworld_kernels.so next to World.x86_64.

Controls are listed along the bottom of the screen. T switches the look.
