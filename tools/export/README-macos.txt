World (macOS, Apple Silicon and Intel)

Run:   unzip, then open World-Godot.app.
       The app isn't notarised, so macOS will refuse it the first time:
       right-click it, choose Open, then Open again (or System Settings >
       Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway"). From a terminal instead:
         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine World-Godot.app

Needs: macOS 11 or newer (Apple Silicon), 10.15 or newer (Intel).

Controls are listed along the bottom of the screen. T switches the look.
